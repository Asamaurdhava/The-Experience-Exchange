Step 1 · Appraise

Anti-Resume Night

What you do
Maya types the experience she'd normally hide. The appraiser taps "Listen for skills," and cards the story points to light up gold.
What happens
Each tapped card writes a line of the read-back ("Maya, what I hear is…") and a drafted resume bullet. Maya never picks her own cards.
Pain point it answers
"I'm very unsure about myself" · "Other people see my strengths better than I do"
Step 2 · Deposit

The Vault

What you do
Maya answers the Friday prompt, "What did you handle this week?" The Vault replies with one follow-up question.
What happens
Her two answers are tagged with skills, credited to her balance, added as a new step on the staircase chart, and logged in the Vault statement.
Pain point it answers
"If I remember it, that's enough" · most students keep no system
Step 3 · Trade

Ticker & Skill Heists

What you do
Maya chooses a skill to teach and one to learn, then joins a 72-hour Heist on a campus problem.
What happens
The board finds a trade partner and a time. Joining a Heist fills a seat and prints a signed Skill Receipt listing the skills she demonstrated.
Pain point it answers
Rejected for "no relevant experience"
Step 4 · Cash out

Market Day

What you do
Maya rings the bell and pitches for 60 seconds, first to a peer, then to a recruiter.
What happens
Her pitch card builds itself from steps 1 to 3 (Context, Action, Result, Reflection). The timer runs two rounds, and the listener's scorecard tracks four checks.
Pain point it answers
Nervous presenting to groups · "How do I explain this so an employer sees its value?"
The website

More than a prototype: a full site.

Built for students on the go.

The site is fully responsive down to phone width, because the weekly deposit is meant to take two minutes on a phone between classes or shifts. It also follows the viewer's light or dark theme.

Under the hood

Simple enough to launch this semester.

BuildOne self-contained index.html: HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no dependencies.
SizeAbout 52 KB. Loads instantly on campus Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Test it yourself

A 2-minute script for judges.

  1. Open the live website and tap Try the prototype.
  2. Step 1: tap Appraiser: listen for skills, then tap any gold-edged card. Watch the read-back and resume bullets rewrite themselves.
  3. Step 2: tap Send twice. The Vault asks a follow-up, credits skills, and adds a new step to the staircase.
  4. Step 3: tap Find a match, then Join this Heist. A signed Skill Receipt appears.
  5. Step 4: read the pitch card built from steps 1 to 3, then tap Ring the bell and start the 60-second round.

Everything shown uses a sample member, Maya R. Campus clients and signatures are examples that illustrate how partnerships would work once the club launches.
