Why it's new

Every career tool tells students to sell themselves. We ban it.

Portfolios, trackers, and resume workshops ask students to judge their own worth, alone and too late. The Experience Exchange runs on four rules no other club at ASU uses.

01

No self-assessment

A peer names your skills first. You edit, you never start from a blank page.

Answers: "others see my strengths better"
02

Skills are the currency

To take a skill, you teach one. No instructors, no budget, every member is both learner and expert.

Answers: "What am I good at?"
03

Proof on demand

72-hour Skill Heists on real campus problems, closed with a signed Skill Receipt instead of an empty resume line.

Answers: "no relevant experience"
04

Shame to strength

The Anti-Resume turns the experience students hide into the story they lead with. Always opt-in and anonymous.

Answers: first-gen and working students
Principled Innovation

Built for the students who get overlooked.

Invisible Work cards give credit to experience that never makes a resume. Everything starts private and one-on-one, and nothing requires sharing a hardship.

Moral

Empathy & humility

We listen before we judge. Every student is named, never forced to sell.

Civic

Intellectual

Reflection
